# Eval: before you deliver

Answer each with yes or no after running `scripts/qa.py` and looking at `qa/contact.jpg` and `qa/opening.jpg`. Any "no" is either fixed or named in your reply.

## Measured (qa.py)

- [ ] Every qa.py check passes, or each failure is explained. The invocation's pinned cast is not a hold: Ganesha and Saraswati dance through it.
- [ ] You looked at the frames either side of every chunk join and every lamp dip.

## Fidelity

- [ ] At least two descriptive sources on the form were read before designing, and are linked in the fidelity note.
- [ ] The invocation opens the play: cast pinned at both screen edges, Ganesha first, then Saraswati.
- [ ] Divinities enter from the audience's right, other characters from the left.
- [ ] Faces never change; every beat is carried by gesture, a bow, a hop or a flip.
- [ ] No maker's puppet was traced; reference photos were used for iconography only.
- [ ] The departures list was kept while designing and appears in the fidelity note.

## Story and source

- [ ] Every scene in story.md has a source reference and an origin (source, invented or mixed).
- [ ] Verses stay in their original script with a rendering beneath.

## Craft

- [ ] **Hook:** the first story scene poses its question within 3 s of the prologue ending, readable with the sound off.
- [ ] **Ending answers the opening:** the last beat resolves the question in `Hook:`.
- [ ] **Readable captions:** at most two lines, under 21 characters a second, all inside the black cloth band.
- [ ] **Always alive:** a hop, a gesture, a lamp dip or an entrance at least every 2–3 s; puppets that hang still for long read as a stalled show.
- [ ] Puppets never collide during bows; props never cross a face.

## Honesty in the reply

- [ ] The fidelity note says what follows the tradition, what departs from it and what was not verified.
- [ ] The reply says plainly that the audio is synthetic, if it is, and recommends crediting the tradition and working with a practising troupe for anything published.
