# Eval: before you deliver

Answer each with yes or no after running `scripts/qa.py` and looking at `qa/contact.jpg` and `qa/opening.jpg`. Any "no" is either fixed or named in your reply as a known weak spot.

## Measured (qa.py)

- [ ] Every qa.py check passes, or each failure is explained (an intended hold missing from `Holds:` counts as explained).
- [ ] You looked at the frames either side of every chunk join in the final file.

## Story and source

- [ ] Every scene in story.md has a source reference, and the origin column says source, invented or mixed.
- [ ] Nothing invented is presented as if it were in the source.
- [ ] Verses stay in their original script, with a rendering beneath.
- [ ] No modern copyrighted translation was pasted.

## Craft

- [ ] **Hook:** by 3 s something on screen poses the story's question, readable with the sound off (open `qa/opening.jpg` and look only at it).
- [ ] **Ending answers the opening:** the last story beat resolves the question in `Hook:`.
- [ ] **Readable captions:** at most two lines, under 21 characters a second (story.py checks this), and nothing important under the caption band.
- [ ] **Always alive:** a visible change at least every 2–3 s (an entrance, a gesture, a camera push, a cut). qa.py fails anything still for 4 s outside intended holds; aim well under that.
- [ ] **One idea per shot:** each shot carries one beat and the eye knows where to look (the speaker, the object, the gesture).
- [ ] **Voiced versions** run at about 2.5 spoken words a second; Indic languages run longer than English for the same line, so retime to the recording.

## Characters and contact

- [ ] Hands are readable, eyes look at what matters, nothing floats.
- [ ] Every contact (a hand on a head, joined hands, a held object, a bow) checked at full resolution.
- [ ] Sacred and traditional subjects drawn with dignity; nothing a tradition does not depict.

## Honesty in the reply

- [ ] The reply says which translations are unproofread, that generated music is synthetic, and whether you watched the whole film or only checked stills.
- [ ] The reply names the one or two weakest spots.
